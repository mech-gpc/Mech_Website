1 · Placements

Placements

Mechanical Engineering has the largest share of the institute’s placement offers. Across seven academic years, the annual counts add up to 145 Mechanical students placed out of 378 final-year students, through 169 offers. Placement rates rose from 23.5% in 2019-20 to a peak of 72.2% in 2024-25. In provisional 2025-26, 10 of 22 final-year students (45.5%) were placed, with a highest package of ₹3.60 lakh.

2 · Internships & Skill Training

Internships & Skill Training

Mechanical students complete a 2-week Internship-I in semester 3 and a 6-week Internship-II in semester 5, mostly with industry. Across six batches from 2022-23 to 2025-26 there were 179 Mechanical participation records against 233 eligible places (76.8%). 177 of 179 took place on-site at the host organisation.

3 · Higher Studies

Higher Studies

Many Mechanical diploma holders continue their studies at engineering colleges and universities. Over nine academic years, the annual counts add up to 281 Mechanical students admitted to higher studies. In provisional 2025-26, 12 students (54.5% of 22 final-year students) did so, all in Mechanical Engineering, across 9 institutions, mostly engineering colleges in Gujarat.

4 · Faculty Development

Faculty Development

Faculty and staff keep their skills current through FDP and STTP courses, NPTEL/MOOC certifications, induction programmes and, since 2024-25, industrial training. The department recorded 142 Mechanical training records between 2019-20 and 2026-27, involving 21 faculty and staff members. That is 48.6% of the 292 records for the whole institute.

Sources and checks

Data notes

Sources

  • Placement Record, 2019-20 to 2025-26 (signed Training & Placement Cell report).
  • Higher Study Report, 2017-18 to 2025-26 (signed).
  • Internship / Training Record, 2022-23 to 2026-27 (NBA Criterion 8.4).
  • Faculty Training Record, 2019-20 to 2026-27 (1 July to 30 June each year).
  • Training & Placement Cell Activity Summary, 2021-22 to 2026-27 (institute-wide).

What was checked

  • Mechanical totals were recounted from the department’s student-level records and matched the report summaries.
  • Spreadsheet and printed-report figures agree for placements, higher studies, faculty training and internships (except 2026-27 skill training).
  • Placement and higher-study lists were compared student by student to find overlap.

What is left out, and why

  • Average packages, because some package entries do not match their monthly figures.
  • 2023-24 highest package, for the same reason.
  • Higher-study percentages for 2019-20 and 2022-23 to 2024-25, because the final-year strength looks too small.
  • 2026-27 skill-based training and Cell activities, which are still in progress and need confirming.

The Training & Placement Office page uses the Criterion 8 presentation, which covers different reporting windows, so its totals differ from the full-period totals above. Academic years run from July to June. Figures marked provisional or partial may change.
